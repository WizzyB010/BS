Privacy Policy

What this site collects, why, and how it's used.

Last updated: September 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers wisdomtech (this website), run by Wisdom Ahiadeke Computing. It describes exactly what the site does with your information — nothing more.

What we collect

  • Theme preference (dark/light): saved in your browser's local storage only. It never leaves your device and isn't personal data.
  • If you sign in with Google to leave a review or reply: we receive your Google display name and profile photo, provided directly by Google after you approve the sign-in. We also store the review or reply text you choose to submit, and your Google account ID (used only to recognize your own reviews — it is never displayed).

We do not collect anything before you take one of these actions yourself. Browsing the site, viewing videos, designs, or templates collects nothing.

Where it's stored, and who can see it

Reviews (name, photo, rating, review text, and any replies) are stored in our Firebase/Firestore database and are displayed publicly on the Reviews section of this site for anyone who visits — that's the point of a public testimonial. Don't post anything in a review you don't want visible to the public.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or advertising trackers run on this site.
  • No cookies are used for tracking or advertising.
  • We never sell your data or share it with third parties.

Buying a template

Paid PowerPoint templates are purchased by messaging us directly on WhatsApp. Any payment details or personal information you share there are handled within WhatsApp itself, under WhatsApp's own privacy terms — this site does not process or store payment information.

Your choices

You can sign out of Google at any time from the Reviews section. To request that a review, reply, or any personal data be corrected or removed, message us on WhatsApp and we'll action it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If what this site collects or how it's used changes, this page will be updated to match. Check back if you're unsure.
